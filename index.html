Shedding Season Notes: Keeping Up With a Double Coat

Our husky mix sheds all year, but twice a year it gets serious. For about three weeks, tufts of undercoat come out every time she shakes, so here is what gets us through.

Brush outside every day, because a good undercoat brush pulls out the loose fur before it lands on the floor. Doing it outside means the fur blows away instead of into the rug.

Keep a hair glove by the couch, where every evening I run it over the cushions in short strokes, and the fur rolls into a ball, so the couch looks clean again in a minute or two.

Vacuum more often, because during the heavy weeks I vacuum every other day instead of once a week, and I empty the canister each time, since it fills fast.

Wash her bedding weekly, and lift the loose fur off with the glove first so less of it goes into the washer.

It is a lot of fur, but it does pass. Check with your vet if the shedding looks unusual or comes with bald spots.
